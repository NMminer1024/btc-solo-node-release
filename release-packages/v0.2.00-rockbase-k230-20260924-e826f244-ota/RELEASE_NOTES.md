### Added
- The About page now has a Storage card that shows the size and usage of every partition on the board (Root FS, Config, Block Data) with a progress bar for each, plus a Total Disk row for the whole card at a glance.

### Changed
- The Dashboard's "Root FS" line was replaced with "Total Storage", which shows how much of the whole card is used. Per-partition details moved to the new Storage card in About.
- The redundant Firmware and System cards on the About page were removed. The "Last Updated" date now lives inside the Firmware Update card.
